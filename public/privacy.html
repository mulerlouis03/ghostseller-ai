GhostSeller AI Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

GhostSeller AI helps users create marketing content, plan campaigns, manage leads, and connect business tools. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect account information such as your email address, name, plan, usage limits, generated content, campaign history, lead data you choose to upload, and technical logs needed to keep the service secure.

2. TikTok Connection Data

If you connect a TikTok account, GhostSeller AI may store OAuth tokens, account identifiers, display name, avatar URL, permissions granted, and publishing attempts. This information is used only to provide TikTok-related features such as connection status, draft upload, and content workflow automation.

3. How We Use Data

  • To provide AI content generation and campaign planning features.
  • To manage your subscription, credits, and usage limits.
  • To support TikTok, Stripe, referral, and acquisition workflows.
  • To improve reliability, security, and customer support.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share limited data with service providers such as hosting, authentication, payment processors, email providers, analytics tools, and connected platforms when required to operate the service.

5. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data. No online service can guarantee absolute security, but we work to reduce risks and protect your account.

6. Data Retention

We keep information as long as needed to provide the service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. You may request deletion of your account data by contacting us.

7. Contact

For privacy requests, contact us at: support@ghostseller.ai
